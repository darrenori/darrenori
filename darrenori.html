Fully Homomorphic Encryption on CUDA

Published with IEEE — a study of how FHE behaves when you throw NVIDIA’s CUDA parallelism at it. Implemented and benchmarked on NVIDIA Jetson Nano devices, comparing public/private key generation, security-bits, default generated key size, and transmission latency. Achieved up to a 95% reduction in computation latency.

Google Air-Gapped Distributed Cloud

Researched Google’s Air-Gapped GDC, focused on sovereign-cloud requirements, security isolation, AI governance and data-residency constraints, and the trade-offs between connected and air-gapped deployments.

Conducted on-site interviews with Andy Lee (Head, Google Cloud Public Sector) and Shang Jiunn Chuah (Customer Engineer, Google Cloud).
